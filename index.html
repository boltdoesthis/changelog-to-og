Paste release notes or a changelog. Get a social card preview, downloadable PNG or SVG, and short tweet / LinkedIn blurbs. Nothing leaves your browser.

What this reads (and how to get a clean card)

Looks for a version (v1.2.0 or ## [1.2.0]), a title or H1/H2, bullet highlights (- / * / •), and an optional date. Keep the top of your notes short: version line, one sentence summary, 3–5 bullets.

  • OG card is drawn at 1200×630 (standard social size). Preview scales down; download is full size.
  • PNG via canvas. SVG is a matching vector export you can edit later.
  • Part of Indie Agent Kit / boltdoesthis free tools.

Tip: Keep highlights short. Cards look best with 3–4 crisp bullets.
